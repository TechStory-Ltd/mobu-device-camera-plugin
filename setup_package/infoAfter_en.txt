
 The OSC Camera Tracker Device plugin is successfully installed.

 Restart MotionBuilder, then drag "OSC Camera Tracker Device"
 from Asset Browser > Devices into the scene.

 Quick start
 1. In the Camera Motion Studio app set up the OSC stream to this
    computer and note its Target Port.
 2. Select the device in the Navigator, set its UDP Port (Communication
    tab) to that Target Port and put the device Online. The phone and
    the computer must be on the same network, and the firewall must let
    UDP in on that port.
 3. In the Model binding bind a camera to the Camera template: it
    follows the phone, with its lens and depth of field.
 4. Tick Recording on the device to key the incoming poses.

 Markers (optional): in the app turn on Settings > Stream > Scene link >
 Accept a marker link, then add models in the Markers tab of the device
 and tick "Send markers to the phone".

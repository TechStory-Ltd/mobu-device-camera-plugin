
 TechStory OSC Camera Tracker Device for Autodesk MotionBuilder.

 This plugin drives a MotionBuilder camera in real time from the OSC
 stream (UDP) of the Camera Motion Studio app - your phone becomes a
 camera tracker.

 * Camera transform, focal length, focus distance and depth of field
   (focus angle), the sensor size of the app as the camera film back.
 * Smooth live motion on Wi-Fi: the poses are placed on the timeline of
   the phone, with an automatic delay that follows the network jitter.
 * Recording of the incoming poses as animation keys, one key per real
   sensor sample.
 * Remote control: the OSC buttons of the app run the MotionBuilder
   transport (play, stop, record, go to start / end, step).
 * Markers: send objects of the scene to the phone to aim at and hold
   focus on (roles Focus, Aim and Ref), and let a Ref object carry the
   live camera, for example a cart or a vehicle.

 Installs for MotionBuilder 2017 - 2027, select the versions on the
 next page. Administrator rights are needed.
